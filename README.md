# 🌱 PALASH — AI-Powered Mother-Tongue Learning Assistant

> An AI-powered offline-first educational platform that bridges the Hindi–Santali language gap in primary education through translation, speech interaction, bilingual learning content, assessments, and personalised remedial learning.

---

## 🏆 Smart India Hackathon 2026

**Problem Domain:** Software / Smart Education

**Focus:** Mother-Tongue Based Multilingual Education (MTB-MLE)

**Initial Language Pair:** Hindi ↔ Santali

PALASH is designed to support teachers and primary-school students in multilingual and low-connectivity educational environments by providing a dedicated language intelligence and learning-support layer.

---

## 🎯 Problem

Many primary-school children in tribal communities learn more effectively in their mother tongue, while educational content and classroom instruction may primarily be available in Hindi.

Teachers may also face difficulties communicating concepts, preparing local-language learning materials, and identifying individual learning gaps.

Existing digital education platforms provide valuable educational content, but there is a need for a dedicated solution that focuses on:

- Hindi ↔ Santali language bridging
- Real-time speech interaction
- Mother-tongue audio learning
- Offline-first classroom support
- Bilingual learning resources
- Student assessment and learning-gap analysis

 Our Solution

PALASH acts as an AI-powered mother-tongue classroom assistant.

The platform converts Hindi educational content into Santali and provides both text and audio support.

It combines:

- 🤖 AI Translation
- 🎙️ Speech-to-Text
- 🔊 Text-to-Speech
- 📚 Bilingual Learning Content
- 📝 Assessments
- 📊 Learning Analytics
- 🎯 Personalised Remedial Practice
- 📱 Offline-First Learning
